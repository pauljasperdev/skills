# The channel

Gemhog tells short business stories for private investors: interesting companies, how they earn their money, why they do what they do, and what could follow from it. The subject is the business world, not Paul's founder journey. An episode runs 60 to 90 seconds and needs no other episode.

Why someone follows, in Paul's words:

> Ich folge Gemhog, weil ich dort spannende Firmen entdecke und verstehe, wie sie ihr Geld verdienen und warum sie bestimmte Dinge tun. Das bringt mich auf neue Gedanken darüber, wie sich die Welt verändern könnte – und wo ich als Anleger genauer hinschauen möchte.

## Zwei Belohnungen

A video pays the viewer twice:

1. **Erste Belohnung:** the title question, answered simply. It gets the room it needs; it is a benefit, not a hurdle before the analysis.
2. **Zweite Belohnung:** one concrete **Börsenbefund** with its main reason. A rounded figure, a fitting comparison and one supported reason can be enough.

A scenario video sets one stated assumption and follows one economic consequence instead. [FORMATS.md](FORMATS.md) says what each format makes of this.

One connection per short. Other interesting finds stay in the Report.

## The viewer

Someone interested in investing who is **no specialist in this topic**. Some hold ETFs and listen to investment podcasts (Doppelgänger, Finanzfluss, Alles auf Aktien); none is assumed to know industry vocabulary.

- Assume interest, not knowledge. Use everyday language; replace a technical term where you can, explain it in passing where you cannot.
- The viewer wants to understand companies, not to buy their products or run their IT. A company's customers are characters in the story.
- A technical detail enters the video only when the chosen insight needs it.

## Topics

Companies, business models, decisions, supply chains and bottlenecks: how does the business work, and why is this position interesting? Or a future scenario: a stated assumption and its plausible economic consequences. AI is the current field, not the limit. A company, a term or a price move alone is not yet a story.

## Limits

- No buy or sell recommendation, no promised return. A reasoned assessment is fine; a fair-value verdict is never required.
- Facts, other people's opinions, own conclusions and assumptions stay distinguishable. Uncertainty is named where it changes the statement.
- The Gemhog podcast app is mentioned only when Paul asks for it.
