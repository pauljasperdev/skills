# Formats

The backlog entry's title is the research question and names the format; the format decides what is researched and what the viewer takes away. The video asks its own question, which `gemhog-script` draws from the Report.

## Was ist eigentlich …?

A term, technology, mechanism or business category. Companies are examples; what holds for one does not automatically hold for the category.

- **Erste Belohnung:** „Jetzt verstehe ich, was der Begriff bedeutet." For a business category: who pays whom for what.
- **Zweite Belohnung:** the hook's answer may be shown on one named company, without becoming a statement about the whole category.
- **Check:** Could a non-specialist explain the term after hearing it once? Is the answer concrete, landed by its deshalb or a punchline, and more than „interessant, aber riskant"?

## Was macht eigentlich …?

One company. Industry traits give only the context this company needs.

- **Erste Belohnung:** „Jetzt weiß ich, was die Firma verkauft und an wen." For a broad group, the part the hook is about, placed in the whole.
- **Zweite Belohnung:** the hook's answer is about *this* company: its business, its share, its next news.
- **Check:** Does the text say what *this* company does? If the answer would also work under a term's title, sharpen the company view.

„Was ist eine Neocloud?" explains the category, also through CoreWeave. „Was macht CoreWeave?" explains CoreWeave's own contracts, financing and position. Two videos need two different insights.

## Was wäre, wenn …?

A thought experiment: set the assumption → play one economic consequence through → say what it could mean for markets and companies.

- **Prämisse:** the assumed change, precise enough to carry the story („es gibt Quantencomputer" is too vague).
- **Folgenstrang:** one chain of consequences. One Prämisse can carry several videos; each takes one strand and introduces the assumption itself.
- **Viewer result:** „Diesen Zusammenhang hatte ich noch nicht gesehen. Jetzt verstehe ich, was unter dieser Annahme wirtschaftlich passieren könnte und wo ich weiter recherchieren würde."
- **Rules:** anchor the story in a researched present-day business or bottleneck. Each step is recognisably a sourced fact, a stated assumption or a reasoned inference. Name the counterforce where it changes the outcome. The ending stays conditional: a business advantage is not yet a statement about a share price. A refuted hunch is also a result.
- **Check:** Is the assumed world clear, and what anchors it today? Are the strand's transitions traceable? Is it a thought about markets and business models, not about technology?
