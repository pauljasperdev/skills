# Writing the hook

The **hook** is the first sentence of a short. In the finished video Paul says it into the camera: a person first, graphics after. Until he records it, the voiceover speaks it as a placeholder, and he rewords it when he has watched the edited video. The hook fixes the video's question; Paul's rewording keeps that question, so the Skript holds.

Paul's **[Short-Form Hook Crafting Guide](https://app.notion.com/p/3cdb424e54608026827fd3c134f4dcd3)** in Notion is the craft: Relevance, Payoff, Tension and Proof; the spoken, visual and text layers; the angles; the construction steps and the final check. Fetch it and work through it. This file adds what holds for Gemhog.

## What Gemhog adds

- **The viewer's questions.** Relevance comes from the questions in [QUESTIONS.md](../gemhog-content/QUESTIONS.md): why it moved, why it fell despite good figures, why nobody can copy it, what could tip it. „Was macht eigentlich X?“ is the research question and stays out of the hook; definitions run weakly (Reuters' „What is SK Hynix and why is it moving the market today?“: 4,600 views), a concrete thing with a puzzle runs strongly („Why is Amazon dumping millions worth of stock?“, How Money Works: 2.6 M).
- **A name the viewer knows.** People invest in what they know, and supplier firms nobody has heard of lack that. The hits on such names borrow it: Apple, Nvidia, the price of a phone, China. The Report's answer to „Woher kenne ich das?“ supplies it.
- **The claim first.** In every transcribed hit on Gemhog's companies, the first sentence states the claim or puzzle. Reveal the what, delay the why: curiosity makes people remember the answer to the question that raised it (Gruber et al. 2014), so the hook asks the question whose answer ends the video.
- **Curiosity is contrast.** The gap between what the viewer expects and what is true makes them stay: a glasses maker behind every AI chip. A surprise that only works for someone who already knows the company is a headline to everyone else.
- **Three layers, one message.** Most viewers watch without sound and read the screen first, so the Text-Hook and the picture weigh more than the sentence. The picture is what happens in the first second: something Paul does or holds (a phone held up, a chip in his hand), and, only where it opens the curiosity loop further, a small card beside him with a logo the viewer recognises or a picture that raises a question (a struck-through brand). The card never adds information: a fact that matters belongs in the Text-Hook. Most hooks need no card; more on screen makes a hook weaker, never stronger. Each candidate is written as all three, and all three say the same thing.
- **Simple enough for a child.** One claim in words a ten-year-old knows, with at most one number, and a round one: neunmal, ein Viertel, 17 Jahre. No subordinate clause, no qualifier, no fine print; a claim that needs a qualifier is not a hook.
- **Ja und?** Read the hook as a sceptic would. When it has an obvious dull explanation, it promises nothing: „Micron liefert weniger Handy-Speicher und verdient mehr“ invites „then they sell something else“. Name the surprise the viewer would not explain away, here that it sells barely more and earns many times as much.
- **Each video stands alone.** Nobody watches two in a row. No layer points to another video or leans on the spoken sentence: no „auch“, „noch“, „wieder“, and a Text-Hook that makes sense with the sound off („Auch das liefert Siemens Energy“ does not).
- **Know today's date.** A short is found months after it is written. A forecast for a period that ends within three months, or a „this year“ that is nearly over, is no hook; what has been reported is.
- **German, in Paul's mouth.** Five to eight words, as the guide says, said without looking, with no word a newcomer would stumble on.
- **Proof from the Report.** The guide's Proof is a sourced fact; every claim in the hook is carried by the Report (SOURCES.md).
- **Lasting, not breaking.** Paul wants a channel people watch in a row, so a short should still hold when someone finds it months later. Lasting hooks rest on how the business works, its structure, a pattern across cycles, or the last year or quarter; breaking ones rest on this week's move or news. Every short ages with the market, so lasting means measured in quarters, not days. A breaking hook is made as a News video that goes out within days, ahead of the lasting ones (Paul, 2026-10-17).
- **Inside the limits.** The hook promises an explanation of what happened or how something works, and its answer stays inside CHANNEL.md's Limits. A question that can only be answered with a verdict on the share („Lohnt sich …?“, „Ist es zu spät für …?“) is no candidate, however well such titles run elsewhere.

The evidence behind these lines is Gemhog's research of 16.10.2026 („Was Privatanleger von Shorts wollen“, `~/Documents/gemhog/recherche/`). It shows directions, not sizes: no study tests finance shorts directly.

## Candidates and their answer

Write each candidate as its three layers, **Gesprochen** (the sentence), **Text** (three to seven words for the screen that make sense on their own, with the sound off, and do not repeat the sentence) and **Bild** (what the viewer sees in the first second: what Paul does or holds while he says it, and, only where it raises curiosity, a recognisable logo or a question-raising picture for the card beside him, never a number or a label line), and with the answer it promises: the payoff the video ends on, landed by its deshalb (QUESTIONS.md) or a punchline. A hook whose answer you cannot write in one sentence is not ready. Vary the candidates across the guide's angles, so the judge compares different promises, not wordings of one.

## The judgement

A fresh judge, who knows only Paul's guide, this file, CHANNEL.md, QUESTIONS.md, the Report and the candidates, runs the guide's final check on each and rates it:

| Criterion | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Lücke | Would an interested non-specialist want the answer, from the hook alone? Strongest where it sets what the viewer expects against what is true; a surprise that needs knowledge the viewer lacks gets at most 1, and a hook with an obvious dull explanation (Ja und?) gets 0. |
| Einlösbar | Does the Report carry the answer, and can a short of 20 to 75 seconds deliver it? |
| Klarheit | Would a ten-year-old understand it at first hearing: every word known, one claim, at most one round number? |
| Anker | Does it hold a name, number or thing the viewer knows? |
| Sprechbar | Five to eight words, natural in Paul's mouth? |
| Ebenen | With the sound off, does each of Text and Bild make sense on its own, without the sentence and without any other video, and do all three layers say the same thing? Each extra element on screen costs a point. |
| Haltbarkeit | Does hook and answer still hold in three months, or only this week? Measured from today's date: a forecast for a period that ends within three months gets 0. |
| Grenzen | Inside CHANNEL.md's Limits, hook and answer alike, and in what the viewer concludes from them? |

Lücke, Klarheit, Anker, Sprechbar, Ebenen and Haltbarkeit each get 0, 1 or 2, with a reason of one line, so a candidate scores at most 12. Einlösbar and Grenzen are gates: a candidate that fails either drops out. For Grenzen the judge writes the one sentence a newcomer would take away from hook and answer; a candidate fails when that sentence lands on cheap or expensive, risky or safe, early or late, though no word of the candidate says so (a KGV explained „kann täuschen“ leaves the viewer at „billig oder teuer“). The judge returns the candidates ranked, the lasting ones first, and for the best candidate of each question the one change to any layer that would make it stronger and the shortest length that still delivers its answer, Punch or Erklärt (craft.md); the writer decides. It also groups the passing candidates by the question they answer: one question is one video, and a Report often carries more than one.

## The hook package

What Paul gets with the edited video, so that recording takes him a minute:

- **Gesprochener Hook:** the winner's sentence, as the placeholder voice says it.
- **Text-Hook:** the winner's Text, which the edit sets as the two pills per its [hooks.md](../gemhog-edit-short/hooks.md).
- **Andere Formulierungen:** three rewordings of the same question, for him to say or to start from.
- **Visueller Hook:** the winner's Bild, as a direction for Paul (what he does or holds while he says the hook) and, where there is one, the card under him (one logo, one number). It goes under Hinweise für den Schnitt.

## More videos from one Report

How many videos a Report yields follows from the judgement, with no cap: every question whose best candidate passes both gates and scores at least 8 of 12 is a video of its own. A different question counts, not a different wording. Its Haltbarkeit is above 0, unless it is a News video with the date after which it no longer holds. `gemhog-script` writes each and puts it on the Videos board as a card of its own.
