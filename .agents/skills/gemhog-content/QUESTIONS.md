# Anlegerfragen

What a private investor asks about a company, and what every Report answers. The list comes from Gemhog's own research of 16.10.2026, „Was Privatanleger von Shorts wollen“ (`~/Documents/gemhog/recherche/reports/`): about 170 Reddit thread titles and 43 threads read on Micron, SK hynix, ASML, TSMC, Oracle, AMD and Siemens Energy, and studies on what draws private investors' attention. In those threads nobody asked what a company makes for its own sake; that question came up as the reason behind a price move, a bottleneck or pricing power.

The questions are the viewer's. Gemhog answers them by summarizing what can be read, inside the limits in [CHANNEL.md](CHANNEL.md).

## Every answer ends in its deshalb

An answer is complete when it says what follows from it for the business or for the next news about it: its **deshalb**. A fact without one is background. „Micron verdient im einen Segment 88 Cent pro Dollar Umsatz, im anderen 76 Cent“ has none. „Der Gewinn ist gestiegen, weil die Preise gestiegen sind, nicht die Menge. Deshalb hängt er jetzt am Speicherpreis“ has one. A deshalb a source carries is stated; one you infer stays a „könnte“ ([SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md)).

## The questions

For a company, all ten. For a term, ask them of the category and the listed companies that carry it; for a scenario, of the companies on its Folgenstrang. A question that does not fit the subject gets one line saying why.

1. **Was ist da gerade los?** The share price over a month and a year, with Stichtag, and the causes reported for the move. Which part was the company's own news, and which moved its sector or the whole market. What investors currently debate about it. Moves without obvious news drew the largest threads („Was habe ich bei ASML verpasst?“).
2. **Was hatte der Markt erwartet?** The last reported figures against the expectations published before them (the company's guidance, the analyst consensus for revenue and profit), and how the share reacted. This is where „gute Zahlen, Aktie fällt“ and „eingepreist“ get their explanation.
3. **Womit verdient sie gerade ihr Geld?** What drives revenue and profit now: price or volume, and which business or customer group carries the growth or the risk. A segment figure enters as an answer to this.
4. **Warum kann das nicht jeder?** Bottleneck, pricing power, the few others who can do it, capacity sold out, what keeps customers. How it differs from the company it is most often compared with. Product detail belongs here when it explains one of these.
5. **Was kommt als Nächstes?** What the company and named sources expect: new product generations, contracts, capacity coming online, the outlook management gave, and the next dated event, usually the next earnings report.
6. **Was könnte die Geschichte kippen?** The risks sources name: the cycle, customers' investment budgets, debt, dependence on a few customers, competition, politics and geography. In the forums this is the most frequent counter-argument.
7. **Wie ist sie bewertet, und warum täuschen die Zahlen leicht?** Current multiples against the company's own history and suitable peers, and what makes them misleading here: a cycle peak, past against expected profit, adjusted against reported figures. Beginners in the threads stumbled exactly here („Was genau bedeutet das?“).
8. **Wie ruhig oder wild war die Aktie bisher, und was zahlt sie aus?** Its largest past falls and rises with their dates and causes; dividend and buybacks. German investors name stability, safety and dividend as selection criteria in surveys (weaker evidence than the forum questions).
9. **Woher kenne ich das?** Where the company touches the viewer's life or names they know: a device, a brand, a customer, a price they noticed. People hold shares of what they know; for a supplier nobody has heard of, this link has to be found.
10. **Was muss ich dafür verstehen?** The terms the answers need, each in one plain sentence with an everyday picture: „eingepreist“, KGV, Zyklus, and whatever this subject brings.
